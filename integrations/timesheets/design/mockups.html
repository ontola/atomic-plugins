Timesheets for Clockify: screens and states

Static mockups for integrations/timesheets/app/, the iframe app. Companion to DESIGN.md in this folder; letters match its §6. Every frame uses only the --pl-* tokens, mapped from the host's --t-* theme variables; the values shown are the host's defaults. Sample data is invented (a designer, Mira Janssen, in workspace Studio Veldkamp; "today" is Thursday 24 September 2026).

Totals are consistent: week 21–27 Sep sums to 26:50 across rows and columns; the 30-day window sums to 94:50.

E Narrow: week strip over one day 360px

Below 560px the grid becomes a seven-day strip (a tablist) above the selected day's entries. The source chip moves into the connection bar; Sync now becomes an icon button with the same accessible name. The right frame is the detail as a full-width sheet.

F–G First run, connecting, workspace and window 560px

F: not connected. G: waiting on the host's consent bar, then choosing a workspace (only shown when the key sees more than one) and the window. Cancelling the consent bar returns to F with no error.
