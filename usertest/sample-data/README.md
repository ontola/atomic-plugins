# Sample data for drive apps

Moderated sessions where the tester needs no provider account (#196). For
four drive apps, `../catalog.mjs` builds a second catalog entry, "<App>
(sample data)", whose module is the app's own built module wrapped by
`proxy.mjs`:

| Entry | Provider fixture (the mock proxy's) | Sample account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `calendar-sample` | `integrations/calendar/fixtures/google-calendar/` | `calendar.mjs`: Acme Studio's calendar around the install day |
| `issue-tracker-sample` | `integrations/issue-tracker/fixtures/github-issues/` (`user-testing` scenario) | `issue-tracker.mjs`: `acme-studio/website`, `brand-guide`, `old-site` |
| `timesheets-sample` | `integrations/timesheets/fixtures/clockify/` | `timesheets.mjs`: Alex Sample, two projects, 14 entries in the last nine days |
| `notion-sample` | `integrations/notion/fixtures/notion/` (`two-sources`) | `notion.mjs`: "Roadmap" and "Reading list", read-only |

The Money app needs no account at all. Its sample bank statements are in
`integrations/money/fixtures/usertest/` (generated by `generate.mjs` there)
and served at `https://catalog.<base-domain>/samples/money/`.

## How it works

- Only `store.proxy` changes. The app runs unchanged, with the host's real
  store for everything else: its table, rows, row access and the review
  steps are the real ones. `proxy.request` goes to the fixture in the
  frame's memory, the same stateful fixture the lanes' e2e tests run behind
  the mock proxy. Nothing leaves the frame for the provider.
- The sample account starts connected. So the app goes straight to its
  picker or first sync on its first mount, which is the path it takes in the
  real host after connecting a new account. `connect` resolves at once and
  `disconnect` works.
- The fixture's state survives the frame being remounted (the tester leaves
  the app and comes back): the seed, the connected flag and every write the
  app sent are kept as JSON in the description of one resource under the
  App, named "Sample <provider> account (user testing)", and replayed into a
  fresh fixture on load, each with `Date` fixed at its original time. A
  fresh install starts over.
- A yellow line above the app's root says the data is invented.
- Every name, place, issue and amount is made up. Keep it that way: these
  files and the plans are public.

## What it can't show

- Signing in to the provider, the consent bar and the proxy: those need a
  real account and the plan without `-sample`.
- Links to the provider's own pages (an event's or issue's page, "Open in
  Notion") lead to pages that don't exist.
- Changes made "on the provider's side" during a session: nobody edits the
  sample account except the app.

## Changing it

Bump `SAMPLE_VERSION` in `../catalog.mjs` whenever this folder or a fixture
it imports changes; the app's own `VERSIONS` bump carries over by itself.
Then:

```sh
node --test usertest/sample-data/samples.test.mjs
node usertest/e2e/samples.mjs
```
